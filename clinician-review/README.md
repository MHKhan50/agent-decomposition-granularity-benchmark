**What she checked:** all 32 cases (16 independent, 16 chained), English and Roman Urdu note side by side with the expected form values, Y/N per question plus a comment in her own words.

| Question | Independent (16) | Chained (16) |
|---|---|---|
| Realistic note? | 0 Y, 16 N | 16 Y |

- **Independent notes not realistic:** every comment names the same line, the patient's brother's age and last year's fee. She judged it a line that would not appear in a real physiotherapy note. That line is the distractor the benchmark adds on purpose.
- **Chained (billing) notes:** realistic, and she found "fee per session" clear in every note when the whole note is read.
- The cases were not changed after the review.
